# Product overview

**Sanskriti AI** helps people experience India's living heritage and gives site
teams a foundation for caring for it. Its product promise is a connected journey
from discovery and understanding to participation and responsible site response.

## Who it serves

| Audience | Need | Product response |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Visitors and travellers | Understand a place in context and navigate the visit | Recognition, Yatrik, narration, monument guides and Explore |
| Students and families | Learn through participation | Quizzes, Treasure Hunt, stories and progress |
| Artisans | Connect cultural traditions with interested visitors | Craft and workshop discovery and reservation workflows |
| Site managers and staff | Review concerns and coordinate accountable action | Site-scoped incidents, review, assignment and audit history |
| Heritage institutions | Evaluate a repeatable visitor and operations service | A bounded pilot with agreed outcomes and integration requirements |

## Visitor journey

Discover a monument → identify or open its guide → ask Yatrik → listen and
explore → complete a learning activity → discover nearby cultural experiences.
A visitor can also voluntarily submit an issue for site staff to review.

The installed clients offer the broadest feature set. The visitor website offers
an installation-free entry point. A deployed NFC/QR checkpoint network, physical
route validation and secure reward validation remain separate roadmap work.

## Management journey

Receive a report → review the evidence → confirm or dismiss → assign a responsible
person → record response and resolution. AI advice must remain distinguishable
from a confirmed finding. Synthetic crowd scenarios rehearse operational decisions;
they do not establish live camera coverage or an accurate occupancy count.

## Implementation status

As documented on 16 November 2026:

- Android and iOS shells package a shared visitor interface with native bridges.
- The visitor website includes Explore, recognition, Yatrik and a hosted-report adapter.
- The MMS includes database-backed site access, incident workflows and AI assessments.
- Local incident and crowd demonstrations coexist with the configured MMS paths.
- Marketplace source includes reservations and payment integration functions;
  local demonstration transactions are not evidence of live commerce.
- Live CCTV ingestion, official ticket validation, physical checkpoint installation,
  operational alert accuracy and institutional pilot outcomes require further work.

The website verification record documents checks from 27 October 2026. It should
not be read as a certification of today's public deployment or physical devices.

## Product principles

1. Keep historical sources, interpretations and AI-generated answers distinguishable.
2. Make language and narration part of the visit, with honest offline behavior.
3. Ask for camera, microphone and location access in the context of an action.
4. Keep staff permissions separate from visitor profiles and marketplace roles.
5. Show unavailable or stale data as unknown; label every simulation.
6. Connect each confirmed incident to an accountable response.
